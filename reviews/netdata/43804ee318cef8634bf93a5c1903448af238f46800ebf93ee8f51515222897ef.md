## Data written by this extension
- `node`: logical name, canonical URL, hostname, OS/version, Netdata version, reachability, fixed error class, transport, capacity/count data, and cloud-claim status. Names, URLs, and host identity are sensitive infrastructure inventory.
- `alarm`: node, alarm name, chart, status, value, units, and free-text information. These may expose operational details.
- `mount`: node, mount path, capacity, utilization, and threshold status. Mount paths may contain usernames or other personal/infrastructure identifiers.
- `summary`: aggregate node, alarm, degradation, and mount counts plus synchronization time; operationally sensitive but not directly personal.
