## Data written by this extension
- `node`: configured name and URL, reachability, classified-or-fallback error, transport, hostname, OS/version, Netdata version, capacity/count data, and cloud-claim state. URL, hostname, error, and platform identity may be sensitive.
- `alarm`: node, alarm name, chart, status, numeric value, units, and free-text info. Names and free text may contain personal or sensitive data.
- `mount`: node, filesystem mount path, capacity, utilization, and threshold state. Mount paths disclose filesystem layout.
- `summary`: aggregate node, alarm, degradation, and mount counts plus synchronization time; no direct personal fields.
