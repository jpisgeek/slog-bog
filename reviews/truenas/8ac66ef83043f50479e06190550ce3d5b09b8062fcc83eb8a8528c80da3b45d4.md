## Data written by this extension
- `system`: hostname, TrueNAS version, hardware model, CPU/memory, uptime, load; hostname is also a tag.
- `pool`: pool name, status, health, capacity, usage, fragmentation; health and status tags.
- `disk`: device name, unique serial number, model, size, type, and pool membership; pool and type tags.
- `alert`: identifier, class, level, full formatted text, dismissal and silencing state; class, level, and silencing tags. Text may contain sensitive operational data.
- `certificate`: certificate name, common name, expiry date, remaining days, and expiry-state flags. Names/CNs may identify private domains.
- `summary`: hostname, version, inventory and health counts, synchronization timestamp; unhealthy-pool and expiring-certificate tags.
