## Data written by this extension
- `system`: hostname, software version, hardware model, CPU/memory/uptime/load metrics. Hostname is identifying.
- `pool`: pool name, status, health, capacity, and fragmentation. Pool names may identify internal configuration.
- `disk`: device name, serial number, model, size, type, and pool membership. Serial numbers are unique device identifiers.
- `alert`: alert ID, class, severity, dismissal state, and free-form text. Text may contain usernames, addresses, paths, serial numbers, or credentials.
- `certificate`: certificate name, common name, expiry, and status. Names and common names may disclose internal domains.
- `summary`: hostname, version, inventory/health counts, degradation flags, and synchronization timestamp.
