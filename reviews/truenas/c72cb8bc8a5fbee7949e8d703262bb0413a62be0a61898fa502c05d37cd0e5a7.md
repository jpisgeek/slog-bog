## Data written by this extension
- `system`: generation ID, hostname, TrueNAS version and hardware model, CPU/memory/uptime/load metrics, and metric-availability state. Hostname and model can identify infrastructure.
- `pool`: generation ID, pool name, health/status, capacity, utilization, fragmentation, and capacity-availability state. Pool names can disclose storage purpose or internal naming.
- `disk`: generation ID, device name, serial number, model, size, type, pool membership, and availability states. Serial numbers and inventory are identifying data.
- `alert`: generation ID, remote alert identifier, class, severity, screened/truncated free-form text, dismissal/silencing state, and content-availability state. Alert text can contain credentials, usernames, addresses, paths, serial numbers, and other sensitive operational data.
- `certificate`: generation ID, certificate name, common name, canonical expiry, remaining days, and expiry states. Names and common names can reveal private DNS and service identities.
- `summary`: generation ID and completeness marker, hostname/version, inventory and health counts, unknown/degraded-state counts, empty-inventory flags, and synchronization timestamp. Hostname remains identifying.
