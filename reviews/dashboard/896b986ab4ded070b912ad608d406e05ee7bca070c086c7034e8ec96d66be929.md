## Data written by this extension
- `exception` resources store normalized producer or coverage condition `id`, severity, subject, headline, detail, source, sensitivity, suppression state/reason, and truncation state. Subject, detail, source, and suppression reason can contain personal, infrastructure, or sensitive operational text supplied by a producer or operator; they are HTML-escaped but not redacted.
- The `render` resource stores the configured output path, HTML byte count, exception/severity counts, bundle counts and IDs, coverage states, exception resource names, and render timestamp. Output paths and bundle IDs can identify local layout or internal systems.
- The self-contained HTML file contains the configured title, validated bundle titles and states, section summaries and coverage reasons, metric labels/values/limits, fact labels/values, exceptions, sources, suppression reasons, and timestamps. Sensitivity metadata is advisory and does not remove or access-control this content.
