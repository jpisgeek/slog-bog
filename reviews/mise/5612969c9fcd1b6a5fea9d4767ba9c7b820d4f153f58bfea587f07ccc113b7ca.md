## Data written by this extension
- `node`: host label, measurement and degradation state, failed probes, transport, mise version, measured directory, drift, and optional error detail. Labels, directories, and error detail may be identifying or sensitive.

- `summary`: aggregate fleet counts and sweep timestamp; no direct personal fields.
